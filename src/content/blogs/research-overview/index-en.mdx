My research connects graph data mining, graph neural networks, event sequence prediction, and foundation models. I focus on putting structural and semantic information into reproducible prediction frameworks.

## Research questions

Event logs contain topology, temporal dependencies, and attribute semantics at the same time. Graphs express interactions, sequence models capture context, and language models offer cross-domain semantic transfer.

## Current directions

I study graph-sequence alignment, semantic-story construction, retrieval-augmented generation, and multi-task prediction for next activity, remaining time, and outcome prediction.
