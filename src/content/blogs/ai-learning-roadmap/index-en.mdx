This is a practical roadmap for learning artificial intelligence, covering mathematics, computer science, Python, machine learning, deep learning, and foundation models.

## Before you start

Learning AI takes sustained practice. Build mathematical, programming, and experimental habits before moving into models, data, and research questions.

## Foundations

Focus on calculus, linear algebra, probability, data structures, operating systems, and Git. Understanding how these tools support model design and reproducible experiments matters more than chasing complex formulas.

## Models and experiments

Start with supervised learning and evaluation, then move through CNNs, RNNs, Transformers, graph neural networks, and retrieval-augmented generation. Keep a runnable experiment record for every topic.
